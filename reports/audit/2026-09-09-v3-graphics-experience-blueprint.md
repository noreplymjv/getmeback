## Executive Summary

GetMeBack Version 2 established a solid foundation: 20 themed rooms, 120 isolated prop sprites, multi-stage health cracking, a 5-weapon demolition arsenal, floor debris physics, gyroscopic parallax on mobile, and a DBT-inspired paced breathing outro.

However, on a global benchmarking scale against top-tier interactive titles (*Teardown*, *Smash Hit*, *Kick the Buddy*, *Room Smash*, *Alto's Adventure*), **Version 2 still displays the limitations of a 2D canvas UI app**. 

**Version 3.0 transforms GetMeBack from a "clickable 2D stage" into a deeply tactile, physics-driven, and cinematic stress-relief simulator.**

## Pillar 1: Procedural Voronoi Texture Shatter

### The V2 Limitation:
In V2, when a prop reaches damage stage 3 (full shatter), the sprite disappears and is replaced by generic colored triangles/splinters generated by `PropShatterController`. A ceramic vase and a porcelain plate break into essentially identical generic geometric chips.

### The V3 Breakthrough:
**Dynamic Image-Texture Slicing (Delaunay/Voronoi Fracture)**:
1. **Epicenter-Aware Slicing:** When the strike lands at `(x, y)` on a prop, generate 12–24 Voronoi seed points clustered around the impact point.
2. **Texture Clipping (`Canvas.drawVertices` / `Path.addPolygon`):** Use the actual bitmap of the prop (`Image.asset`), crop it along the Voronoi polygon boundaries, and spawn 12–24 individual image shards.
3. **Specular Edge Beveling:** Draw a 1px bright edge highlight on each piece to simulate the cut refractive edge of real shattered glass, ceramic, or splintered wood.
4. **Result:** The user literally sees the *actual object* break apart into realistic jagged pieces.

## Pillar 2: Real Flutter GLSL Fragment Shaders

Flutter 3+ natively supports compiling SPIR-V fragment shaders (`.frag`) executed directly on the GPU (via Impeller or Skia).

### Three High-Impact Shaders for V3:

#### A. Shockwave Refraction Shader (`shaders/shockwave.frag`)
* **Trigger:** Heavy strikes (Wrecking Ball, Power Fist, Earthquake).
* **Effect:** An expanding refractive ring radiates from the epicenter for 350ms, bending and distorting the underlying background room art like a supersonic blast wave.
* **Why it feels 10/10:** It transforms a flat tap into a devastating, screen-bending blast.

#### B. Impact Chromatic Aberration (`shaders/chromatic_aberration.frag`)
* **Trigger:** Critical hits and weapon impact frames.
* **Effect:** For 40 milliseconds during hit-stop, red and blue color channels diverge slightly (RGB split) across the impact zone, mimicking high-speed cinematic action cameras.

#### C. Volumetric Lighting & Ambient Dust (`shaders/volumetric_light.frag`)
* **Trigger:** Ambient idle state in all 20 rooms.
* **Effect:** Replaces simple timer-based floating dots with realistic atmospheric light beams streaming from windows, with micro-motes of dust catching the light in a natural flow field.

## Pillar 3: Directional Swipe & Kinetic Physics

### The V2 Limitation:
Tapping a weapon in the HUD simply activates a stationary strike animation centered directly on the prop's anchor.

### The V3 Breakthrough:

#### A. Velocity-Based Swipe Mechanics
* **Baseball Bat & 2x4 Lumber:** The user swipes across the screen. The angle of the swipe dictates the bat's swing trajectory; the swipe velocity dictates the impact force.
* **Directional Scatter:** Swiping left-to-right knocks props toward the right wall, bouncing shards in the direction of the swing vector.

#### B. Kinetic Telekinesis / Prop Dragging
* Long-pressing any intact prop (e.g., a coffee mug or bowling ball) lifts it with an organic shadow lift.
* Flinging it into another prop causes a **dual collision**: both props take damage, creating organic, emergent chain reactions.

#### C. Environmental Chain Reactions
* Smashing a table or shelf destabilizes any items resting on top of it, causing them to tumble, roll, and shatter upon hitting the floor baseline.

## Pillar 4: Dynamic Lighting & Real-Time Shadows

### The V2 Limitation:
Room backgrounds have pre-baked lighting. Props look pasted on because they do not cast shadows relative to dynamic light sources.

### The V3 Breakthrough:
1. **Dynamic Drop Shadows:** Instead of static ellipse shadows, calculate the shadow offset and blur radius based on the room's primary light vector (e.g., ceiling fluorescent in the office, sunbeam in the living room).
2. **Strike Flashes:** When the Laser or Explosion strikes, render a 150ms radial additive point light (`BlendMode.plus`) that illuminates the edges of nearby props and casts momentary elongated shadows across the room floor.
3. **Bullet-Time Finisher:** When the 20th and final prop in a room is pulverized, the app smoothly slows time to 0.25x speed for 1.2 seconds, pulls camera zoom to 1.1x with a subtle chromatic vignette, and lets the final debris settle in cinematic slow-motion.

## Pillar 5: Acoustic & Audio Engine 3.0

Graphics feel 50% better when paired with hyper-tactile audio.

### The V3 Audio Upgrades:
1. **Micro-Pitch & Velocity Randomization:**
   * Every time glass or ceramic breaks, randomize the sound pitch by `±7%` and slightly vary the playback volume. This guarantees that 10 consecutive smashes never sound synthetic or repetitive.
2. **Spatial Stereo Panning:**
   * Props broken on the far left of the screen play with a `left: 0.85, right: 0.15` stereo balance; props on the right pan accordingly.
3. **Secondary Settle Sounds (Tinkle Layer):**
   * 150ms after the primary heavy impact sound, play a soft, randomized high-frequency "glass clinking / wood roll" settle audio loop.
4. **Sub-Bass Rumble:**
   * Pair the Wrecking Ball and Earthquake with a low-frequency 50Hz sub-bass thump that triggers heavy device motor haptics.

## Pillar 6: Zen 3.0 — The Emotional Catharsis Evolution

GetMeBack's greatest psychological differentiator is the bridge from **Venting** to **Calming**. In V3, this transition becomes deeply rewarding:

### A. Kintsugi Restoration Mode (Emotional Rebuilding)
* After the rampage is complete and the breathing phase finishes, offer an optional **"Kintsugi"** ritual.
* The user selects one broken item from the room. With calming ambient chimes, they drag glowing liquid-gold lacquer across the fracture seams to repair the object.
* **Psychological Impact:** Moves the user from destructive release to restorative empowerment (*"Broken things can be put back together stronger than before"*).

### B. Tactile Paper Shredder Journal
* Replace the standard text dialog with a photorealistic virtual notepad.
* The user writes or dictates their exact rage or frustrating thought onto the paper.
* They drag a mechanical lever down, and the paper physically feeds into spinning industrial shredder teeth with satisfying crunching audio and paper ribbon debris.

### C. Binaural Ambient Soundscapes
* During the 4-second inhale / 4-second exhale cycle, play generative 432Hz ambient solfeggio tones with gentle nature soundscapes (ocean swell, autumn rain) that swell on the inhale and soften on the exhale.
